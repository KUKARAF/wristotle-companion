Three additions and two fixes:

- Ask Agent → Anthropic gains a "Web search (Claude
  built-in)" toggle. When on, Claude can search the web
  mid-reply for fresh facts (news, sports, prices) and
  return a cited answer. Off by default — Anthropic
  bills per search.
- App aliases now handle compound words automatically:
  "audiobook" matches when dictation transcribes "audio
  book" (and vice versa). No need to add both spellings.
- App aliases card has a new how-to-add guide in its info
  blurb.

Fixes:
- "remind me in two hours to <task>" no longer keeps
  "in two hours" in the reminder title.
- The Ask Agent system-prompt hint points users on
  reasoning models (Qwen3 / DeepSeek-R1) at the server
  flag for chain-of-thought leakage.
