Color theme + watch polish + chat bubbles.

Settings → 📱 Watch gains a new "Color theme"
switch (off by default). When on, color-capable
Pebbles (Time / Time Steel / Time Round /
Time 2 / Emery) light up:

- Colored menu icons — red bell, green check,
  amber page, blue mic, purple gear.
- LightGray menu highlight so icons stay
  visible on the selected row.
- Pastel-yellow chat bubbles.
- Accent-yellow progress strip during
  dictation / agent activity.

B&W watches stay monochrome regardless.

Companion-side change for the watch v1.1.0
polish pass — design tokens, per-row icons,
rounded chat bubbles, and the Quick chooser
dictation race-fix all live on the watch.

Ships alongside watch v1.1.0.
