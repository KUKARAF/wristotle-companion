Morning brief.

Ask "morning brief" or "what's on my
plate today" and Wristotle reads back a
one-shot rollup of today's meetings,
alarms, reminders, pending tasks, notes
created today, and unread messages from
your messaging apps.

Tight format for the watch chat — each
section gets a short line, capped to ~280
characters total. Empty sections drop
out; long days truncate from the bottom
so the time-anchored items stay visible.

Unread messages read live from
notification access (no persistence).
A notification you've already dismissed
won't appear in the brief.
