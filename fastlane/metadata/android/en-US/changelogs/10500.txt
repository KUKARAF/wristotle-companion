Persistent reminders.

Some reminders you really don't want
to miss. Ask for "persistent reminder
to take meds at 9pm", "nag me to call
mom at 5", or "keep reminding me to
drink water at noon".

The watch timeline pin still fires
at the scheduled time. Your phone
then keeps re-firing a notification
every 10 minutes (configurable —
Settings → Reminders → Persistent
reminders) until you tap Stop, run
out of nags, or cancel the reminder
by voice.

Tap Stop on the notification to kill
the whole chain. Tap the body to
acknowledge this one but keep being
reminded. Cancel a persistent reminder
by voice and the phone-side chain
clears too — no chasing the
notification.
