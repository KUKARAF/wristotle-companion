Morning brief catches dismissed
notifications.

The brief's "Messages" line used to read
only your current tray — anything you
swiped before brief time was gone.

New opt-in toggle in Settings → 🔔
Notifications → Include dismissed
notifications. When on, Wristotle stores
app id + conversation key + timestamp
for every notification posted today, so
the brief still surfaces what you'd
already cleared. Body, title, and sender
are never read or stored. Auto-prunes
after 7 days; Clear log wipes it on
demand.

The brief also shows raw message count
alongside the conversation count when
they diverge: "Messages: 1 conversation
(5 msgs) from WhatsApp" for chatty
threads.
