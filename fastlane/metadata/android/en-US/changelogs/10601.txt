Internals: KMP refactor.

Lifted the non-Android logic of the
companion (handlers, NLU pipeline,
settings storage, LLM clients, MCP
client, watch transport, ~170 files
in all) into a new :wristotle-core
multiplatform module that builds for
both Android and iOS Simulator.

No user-visible changes — same
features, same UI, same wire shape
to the watch. Foundation work for a
future iOS port; the Android app on
your phone behaves identically to
v1.6.0.

Twelve platform-interface seams
(KeyValueStore, Logger, HttpClient,
WatchTransport, Telephony,
AlarmScheduler, Notifier,
CalendarReader, ContactsResolver,
AppLauncher, FileSystem, TimeParser)
keep Android-only code on the
Android side; everything else lives
in commonMain and compiles for iOS
in CI on every commit.
