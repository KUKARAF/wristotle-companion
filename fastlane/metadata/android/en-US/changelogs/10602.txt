Voice fixes — four bug fixes
landed in voice handling.

• Sent text messages now keep the
  trailing ? or ! you said. Saying
  "text dad did you have lunch?" no
  longer sends "did you have lunch".

• SetTimer recognises 11-19 and the
  compound tens (twenty-one through
  ninety-nine) as word-form durations.
  "Set a timer for eleven minutes"
  used to return "Couldn't understand
  the duration"; now it works (#11).

• Same word-number coverage extends
  to Reminder, SetAlarm, Reschedule,
  CreateEvent, and Calendar — anything
  that flows through the time parser.
  "Set an alarm for thirteen minutes"
  works for the first time.

• "Cancel timer" now points you at
  the Clock app instead of insisting
  there's no reminder by that name.
  Android has no public DISMISS_TIMER
  intent, so cancelling a timer has
  always required the Clock app —
  this just tells you that honestly.
