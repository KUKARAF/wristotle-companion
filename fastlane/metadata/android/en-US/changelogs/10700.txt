Speak replies on the watch.

Experimental: have the watch read
replies aloud through its built-in
speaker. Only Pebble Time 2 and
Pebble Round 2 have a speaker —
every other model drops the audio.

Settings → Speech. Off by default.
Pick which intents speak (Reminders,
Tasks, Notes, Ask Agent, Morning
brief, etc.) and pick an engine:

• Local — phone's Android TTS.
  No network, ~4 s to first audio.
• Cloud — any OpenAI-compatible
  /v1/audio/speech endpoint. ~1 s
  to first audio.
