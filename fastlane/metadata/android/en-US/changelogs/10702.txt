SetAlarm bug fixes (closes #12).

• "Set an alarm for one hour and
  four minutes from now" used to
  return "Couldn't understand the
  time". Now sums compound durations
  the same way SetTimer always did.

• "Set an alarm for 7:51 p.m."
  parses the same as "7:51 pm" —
  the dots in a.m. / p.m. no longer
  break the time parser.

SetTimer + SetAlarm now share one
duration-parsing helper so they
can't drift apart again.
