Speech card test buttons + stream
hardening.

The "Speak on watch" and tone test
buttons on Settings → Speech were
producing garbled audio because they
skipped a pre-warm step real voice
replies always sent. Now they mirror
the dispatch path.

Plus defensive stream cleanup: the
watch's TTS stream now always closes
on every exit (success, synth fail,
BLE drop, cancellation), so the watch
can never get stuck mid-stream
waiting for a close that never came.
