On-device activity stats.

New Settings → Stats card surfaces
what you've been asking Wristotle
for and how well it understood.
Everything is computed locally —
nothing leaves your phone.

Six panels: streak, top intents
(30d), when you talk to Wristotle
(24-hour chart), accuracy (30d +
lifetime + NLU confidence), where
Wristotle stumbles (top three to
teach), and notes / tasks /
reminders / alarms on your phone.
