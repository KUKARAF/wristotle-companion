Voice scheduling fixes.

Voice alarms work again ("set an
alarm for 5 minutes"). A bare hour
now means the next one — at 2:30pm,
"remind me at 8" means 8pm today —
applied consistently whether you
create, reschedule, cancel, or ask.

Reminder and meeting titles are
cleaner: a time-only reminder is
titled "Wristotle Reminder" instead
of "8pm", and "with Alex" stays as
the attendee.
