Formula 1 and cricket.

Sports now covers Formula 1 and
cricket alongside soccer and the
US major leagues.

F1: next race, last-race podium,
and driver / constructor standings.
Say "when is the next f1 race",
"who won the grand prix", or
"f1 standings".

Cricket: scores, results, and the
points table for the IPL and World
Cup. Say "ipl score", "did rcb
win", or "ipl standings".

Plus: switch off any sport you
don't follow in Settings → Sports —
it won't show up or match team
names. Favorites, sport priority,
and these on/off choices all travel
in Backup & Restore.
