Replies as cards on the watch.

Sports, weather, and meeting replies
now show as full-screen cards — a
coloured band for the action and an
icon for the feature — with a rich
weather card (temperature, condition,
humidity, wind).

Turn cards on or off per type in
Settings → Watch. Reminders, tasks,
notes, alarms, the agent, and the
morning brief can all be switched on
there too.
