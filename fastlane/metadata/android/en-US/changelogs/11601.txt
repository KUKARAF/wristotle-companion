Fixes and watch tweaks.

- Custom wake words: you can now add more than one (the
  field wouldn't take a second line before). Works for
  Home Assistant and Ask Agent.
- New "Code display time" setting (Settings > Watch): how
  long a voice-recalled code stays on the watch before it
  auto-closes, or keep it up until you press Back.
- Bind a watch button to your saved codes (Settings >
  Watch > shortcuts > Codes).
- Voice events/reminders that name an explicit date (e.g.
  "July 29th at 3pm") no longer drift to the wrong day.
